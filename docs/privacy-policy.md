# Apples to Apples Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

## Summary

Apples to Apples reads product and price information from shopping pages to
calculate comparable unit prices. Processing happens on your device. We do not
operate a data-collection service, and the extension does not send page
contents, browsing activity, or calculations to us or to third parties.

## Information Processed

While an HTTP or HTTPS page is open, the extension may temporarily read visible
product names, prices, package quantities, retailer unit prices, page structure,
and the page hostname. This information is used only to identify shopping
results, calculate normalized unit prices, display those results on the page,
and support user-requested sorting.

## Information Stored

Display-unit choices and extension settings are stored locally through Chrome
extension storage. Product information, page contents, URLs, and calculated
prices are not retained after the page or browser context is closed. Removing
the extension removes its locally stored settings.

## Sharing And Selling

We do not sell user data. We do not share user data with advertisers, data
brokers, analytics providers, model providers, or other third parties. The
extension contains no telemetry, advertising, affiliate-link replacement,
account system, or remote model call.

## Permissions

- Site access lets the extension recognize shopping results on familiar and
  unfamiliar websites.
- Scripting lets the user request a fresh scan from the extension popup.
- Storage keeps extension preferences locally on the device.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web
Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes And Contact

Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and extension release.
Questions can be directed to the developer through the
[publisher profile](https://github.com/erichasinternet).
